  • parser. The shared parser reads every date month first, and every tenant here writes the day first. Day and month swap: 7 April becomes 4 July. This is the bug #4471 names.
  • hint. booking-v3’s form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field, so people type the month first, and the form reads the value day first. A correct parser still gets these wrong. Only tenants on booking-v3 who book through the form see it.
  • shift. The date moves by one day, Tuesday to Monday, and the day and month do not swap. One meeting reached Lisbon as Tuesday and Toronto as Monday. This is time-zone handling. The desk already read these as a one-day move (desk-notes.json), but the #4471 page still counts them under parser.

How the counts are made

  • Reports are counted by id. The ticket export overlaps, so some reports appear twice.
  • A report’s problem comes from its tenant’s locale and booking form, plus the desk’s notes on the body. Intake’s tag is a guess from the reporter’s browser. It misfiles reports both ways, so nothing here counts by it.
  • Nothing is typed in. The table is rebuilt from the export on every build, so new reports land in the right row. A report no rule places shows up as unplaced with no ticket.

Since April

Lessons

  • Matching on subject merged three problems into one ticket (decision 0001). The subject says what a person saw. The cause comes from their tenant and from what the body says.
  • Before you mark a big ticket known, check whether one fix closes every report in it.
